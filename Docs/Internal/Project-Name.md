# Project Name: VEGA

## Name & Expansion

**VEGA**: **V**ariance **E**valuation & **G**raphical **A**nalytics

> A LAN-served **Budget vs Actual** tracker: it imports a department's fiscal-year budget and monthly General Ledger from Excel, then reports the variance and the year-end projection.

**Tagline:** *"Every number, clear as starlight. Every insight, connected like a constellation."*

## Origin & Meaning

**Vega** is the brightest star in the constellation **Lyra** (the lyre/harp). Three readings of the name line up with what the app does:

- **Star.** Vega was the first star other than the Sun ever photographed, and for decades it was the zero point of the magnitude scale: the fixed reference every other brightness was measured against. A budget plays the same role: the reference figure the actuals are measured against.
- **Lyre / harp.** An instrument is only useful once it is *in tune*. The app's whole job is checking whether reality (actuals) is still in tune with the plan (budget), and flagging the moment it drifts.
- **Finance term.** "vega" is a genuine options *Greek*, measuring sensitivity to volatility. The name reads as credible in a financial context rather than decorative.

The metaphor fits the app directly: make every financial number **"clear as starlight"** and connect insights **"like a constellation"**. That is clarity plus connected analytics, the job of a Budget vs Actual dashboard.

**Impression:** credible, catchy, stellar.

## Scope of the Name

**VEGA is the whole project. There is no second component.** An anomaly-detection
study (TRACER-AI) was considered early on and dropped from the project entirely, so
nothing else carries a name here.
